## Main Idea
- **Bloom’s Taxonomy** is basically a **mental framework** for **how humans think and learn**, structured like a **pyramid** — where each level represents a **deeper, more complex form of cognition**
	- To **ascend the pyramid** means to move from **simple information handling** to **mastery, creation, and critical thinking** — it’s the journey from being **a passive receiver** of facts to becoming a **powerful thinker, creator, and strategist**.
	- An application of what it means to think actively according to [[The 4 Pillars of Learning]]
	- ![[Screenshot 2025-02-13 at 18.08.10.png]]
- Implication
	- Most people **stay stuck at Levels 1–2** (remember, understand) — they **pass exams**, but they **can’t think flexibly or critically**
	- To **excel in uni, career, life** — you need to **operate at Levels 4–6** consistently
